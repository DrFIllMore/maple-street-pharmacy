## v25.27 · 2026-10-30 — Level 3: The Three Letter Store
- **Level 2 no longer ends the game.** Trust 90, $100k net and a working drive-thru now open **Level 3**: a chain called **The Three Letter Store** opens across the street. A 9-second Channel 9 breaking-news scene pans from your store across the street as their sign goes up, a reporter on the sidewalk, the manager stepping out to fold their arms. The building stays on the map (outside view) with its own drive-thru and clinic when they add them.
- **The block is shared.** Your share = 100% − their pull. Their pull starts around 45% and settles in over a month, grows with their moves and shrinks with yours: trust above 80, your drive-thru, a price list below fair, promotions (price-match week especially), the delivery van, loyalty tiers, good press. Their share also takes that many walk-ins (half of it), so losing the block costs real money.
- **Their move each week** (about two weeks in three): a 10% price cut for three weeks, their own drive-thru (permanent), a walk-in clinic (permanent, takes a fifth of shot seekers), an insurer steering deal (four weeks) — or a bad week of their own (their share drops, your trust +1). No poaching of your staff. The Saturday card, the Monday log and the Promote page say what they did.
- **New levers in Promote at Level 3:** a **Delivery van** ($4,000 + $120/wk: regulars grow 25% faster, −6% for TLS; it parks on your apron) and **Loyalty Silver / Gold** ($800 / $1,500 on top of Loyalty cards: regulars +20%, −3% each, Gold adds +1 trust a week).
- **Win:** hold **60% of the block for four weeks running** with trust 90 and no bank strikes → a closing Channel 9 scene (crowd outside your store, FOR LEASE across their sign) and the Level 3 complete page. **Lose:** they hold half the block four weeks running → buy-out.

## v25.25 · 2026-10-30 — Seasons, challenges, setbacks
- **Seasons.** A 13-week cycle: Autumn (3 weeks, steady), Winter (4: shots +50%, sick days +40%, walk-ins −3%), Spring (3: walk-ins +8%, OTC shoppers +40%), Summer (3: walk-ins −10%, staff tire 10% slower, shots rare). The proposals page shows the season; the summary card and Monday's log announce a new one.
- **Challenges.** From week 2, when you have none running, one of the proposals is a one- or two-week contract with a target and a reward: fill N prescriptions (clinic, cash), fewer walk-outs two weeks running (district office, trust +3), N flu shots in two weeks (county, $800), no call unanswered ($400), sell $N over the counter ($500), nobody under 40% energy (team morale +8), clear every open claim ($600). Targets come from your last week. No penalty for missing one. Progress shows as a blue line on every office page during the week, in the proposals while it runs, and the result on the Saturday card (+60 XP when met).
- **Designed setbacks**, as required decisions with a trade-off: a SilverPlan **claims audit** (open claims cost $100 each; pay a $400 fee or do it in-house with clerks 20% slower that week), a **drug recall** (15% of stock goes back: take the wholesaler's credit and run light, or $500 for a rush replacement), and a **state inspection** of the consult room (prepare for $300, or say you're ready: a fridge and doses in stock pass for +2 trust; a fail closes the room for the week, −1 trust). Each has a cooldown of 6–8 weeks.
- The desk shows three proposals at most, so it never scrolls: when a challenge is offered, the routine staff note steps aside (a real staff request does not).

## v25.16 · 2026-10-29 — Fix: the drive-thru starved, and the week now flags payroll
**What happened (your week 21–23 report):** the week before the drive-thru: 468 served, 12 lost. The week it opened: 241 served, 249 lost. Every lost customer was a car at the drive-thru, while clerks sat idle. I rebuilt your store and lane from the report and reproduced it: **0 cars served at the window, 116 gave up.**
- **Why:** your lane wraps around three sides of the store (64 tiles). The game picked the **longest** way in (44 tiles), to make room for a long line. Cars crawl through a lane, and their patience counted down while they were still driving, so they gave up before reaching the window. Those cars then blocked the lane for everyone behind them. Half your pick-ups were sent to that lane.
- **Fixed:**
  - Cars now take the **shortest** way in.
  - Patience only counts down while a car is **stuck in line**, not while it's moving.
  - When **6 cars** are already waiting, new pick-ups come inside to the counter instead.
  - Same store and lane: **51 served at the window, 0 gave up, 336 served in all** (was 228).
- **The drive-thru window's info** now shows cars served and cars that gave up this week, and how many are in line now.
- **Payroll warning at week end:** when wages are more than 55% of what the week earned, the money messages say so, name how many of each role sat idle more than half the week, and say what letting them go would save. In your store, 4 of 7 pharmacists were idle 74–98% of the week. Only 2 verify terminals means only 2 pharmacists can check at a time.

## v25.5 · 2026-10-28 — Bug sweep and speed-up after the v25 updates
**Bugs fixed** (found by a 24-week soak test with every feature on, and an independent code review)
- **Reloading a game with a driveway broke the game.** The driveway lookup was saved into the game file, came back empty, and threw an error every frame. It's no longer saved, and old saves are cleaned up on load.
- **The drive-thru stopped working after the store grew** (0 cars a week from then on). The driveway lookup didn't notice that every tile had moved. It now refreshes whenever the lot changes.
- **A dead-end lane jammed the drive-thru.** If cars had to come back out the way they went in, a car leaving the window and the car waiting behind it blocked each other (one test week: 3 served, 51 lost). The lane now needs a separate way out, and the build hint says so.
- **Drive-thru customers got in-store events.** Cars at the window could "use the restroom", buy snacks, wander into the pharmacy, or be sick on the pavement outside, leaving a mess no one could reach that kept draining trust. These events now only happen inside.
- **Growing the store north or south could push driveway tiles onto the parking rows and storefront sidewalk.** Those tiles now come up for half back.

## v24.31 · 2026-10-27 — Rx pricing, borrowing, the foreclosure check
- **Cash price is open: $30–$120** (was $36–$60), in $4 steps (Supplies). The **fair price** is $48 at trust 50 and moves $2 per 10 trust.
